## 2. Organisational overview

True Era Sustainable Response Uganda (TESR-U) is a nationally
registered, community-centered, non-profit organisation dedicated to
promoting sustainable development, environmental stewardship, resilience
building, and social transformation among vulnerable populations across
Uganda.

Established in 2024 and registered with the Uganda Registration Services
Bureau (URSB) under Registration Number 80044333317562, TESR-U operates
as a company limited by guarantee without share capital, committed to
delivering sustainable and community-driven development solutions.

The organisation was founded in response to persistent socioeconomic,
environmental, and humanitarian challenges affecting communities across
Uganda. It empowers individuals and communities to become active
participants in shaping their own development. TESR-U believes
sustainable change is achieved through local ownership, inclusive
participation, innovation, and strategic partnerships that strengthen
resilience and improve livelihoods.

TESR-U currently implements programmes across Northern Uganda, including
Lamwo, Kitgum, Agago, Omoro, Gulu, and Kiryandongo, while progressively
expanding its reach to the West Nile sub-region and other underserved
areas. Through its grassroots approach and community presence, TESR-U
has built trust among local communities, government institutions, civil
society organisations, and development partners.

Headquartered in Kiryandongo District, TESR-U operates within Uganda's
development and humanitarian landscape, serving both host and refugee
communities through integrated, sustainable, and locally led
interventions.

## 5. Core values

### 5.1 Community-Centered

TESR-U places people at the heart of every intervention, ensuring that
communities actively participate in identifying challenges and shaping
solutions.

### 5.2 Sustainability

TESR-U promotes long-term and environmentally responsible solutions that
improve lives without compromising the wellbeing of future generations.

### 5.3 Integrity

TESR-U upholds transparency, accountability, professionalism, and
ethical conduct in all its actions and partnerships.

### 5.4 Collaboration

TESR-U believes transformational change is achieved through strategic
partnerships, collective action, and shared responsibility.

### 5.5 Innovation

TESR-U embraces creativity, learning, and adaptive approaches to address
emerging development and humanitarian challenges.

### 5.6 Equity and Inclusion

TESR-U ensures meaningful participation and equitable access to
opportunities and services for women, men, youth, persons with
disabilities, refugees, and other marginalised groups.

### 5.7 Resilience

TESR-U strengthens the capacity of communities and institutions to
withstand, adapt to, and recover from social, economic, and
environmental shocks.

## 6. Strategic focus areas and programme content

The profile identifies six thematic areas. These should form the core of
the website's Programmes/Our Work section.

### 6.1 Climate Change Adaptation and Environmental Conservation

**Description:** TESR-U promotes sustainable environmental management
and climate-resilient practices that enable communities to adapt to
climate change while protecting natural resources.

**Key interventions** - Climate-smart agriculture. - Tree planting and
reforestation. - Sustainable land management. - Natural resource
conservation. - Renewable energy solutions. - Environmental awareness
and advocacy.

**Potential website content components** - Programme overview. -
Intervention list. - Relevant field photographs and project stories,
once supplied. - Related locations and beneficiaries, where verified.

### 6.2 Sustainable Livelihoods and Economic Empowerment

**Description:** TESR-U supports vulnerable households to improve their
incomes, build assets, and achieve economic self-reliance.

**Key interventions** - Agricultural value-chain development. - Village
Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs). - Entrepreneurship and business
development. - Financial literacy training. - Market systems
development. - Youth employment and skills development.

### 6.3 Youth Empowerment and Skills Development

**Description:** Recognising the potential of Uganda's young population,
TESR-U invests in programmes that equip youth with skills,
opportunities, and confidence to become drivers of positive change.

**Key interventions** - Vocational skills training. - Leadership
development. - Innovation and entrepreneurship. - Employability
enhancement programmes. - Digital literacy initiatives. - Youth
participation in governance and development.

### 6.4 Protection and Social Inclusion

**Description:** TESR-U works to ensure that vulnerable individuals and
groups live free from violence, exploitation, discrimination, and abuse.

**Key interventions** - Child protection. - Gender-Based Violence (GBV)
prevention and response. - Community-based protection systems. -
Referral and case management. - Rights awareness and advocacy. - Social
inclusion programming.

### 6.5 Peacebuilding, Social Cohesion and Community Resilience

**Description:** TESR-U supports communities affected by conflict,
displacement, and social tensions to build peaceful, inclusive, and
resilient societies.

**Key interventions** - Peacebuilding and conflict resolution. -
Community dialogue platforms. - Social cohesion initiatives. -
Refugee-host community integration. - Resilience strengthening
programmes. - Local governance engagement.

### 6.6 Humanitarian Response and Recovery

**Description:** TESR-U delivers timely and accountable humanitarian
assistance while supporting long-term recovery and resilience building.

**Key interventions** - Emergency preparedness. - Disaster risk
reduction. - Food security support. - Emergency livelihoods recovery. -
Community-based response mechanisms. - Protection in emergencies.
