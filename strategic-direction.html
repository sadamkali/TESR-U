Strategic Direction 2027–2032

TESR-U's organisational profile sets out a forward-looking direction for 2027–2032. The priorities below describe intended areas of focus, not completed achievements.

01
Geographic Expansion

Expand operations into West Nile and other underserved regions.

02
Governance and Institutional Systems

Strengthen organisational governance and institutional systems.

03
Funding and Partnerships

Diversify funding and partnership opportunities.

04
Programme Quality

Increase programme quality and evidence-based programming.

05
Climate Resilience

Scale climate resilience and environmental sustainability initiatives.

06
Women and Youth Economic Empowerment

Enhance economic empowerment interventions for women and youth.

07
Protection and Social Cohesion

Promote protection, peacebuilding and social cohesion.

08
Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning

Strengthen monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning systems.

09
National Positioning

Position TESR-U as a leading national organisation delivering locally led development solutions.

Institutional Alignment

The profile states that TESR-U's work aligns with Uganda Vision 2040, the Sustainable Development Goals and the African Union Agenda 2063. It does not specify individual SDG targets or measured contributions.
