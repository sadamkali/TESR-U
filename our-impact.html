Our Impact

Working Alongside Communities

TESR-U's organisational profile describes work with communities across Northern Uganda and an ambition to extend support to other underserved areas. This page presents the current geographic areas and community groups identified in that profile.

Areas Listed in the Organisational Profile

TESR-U's profile identifies the following current programme areas:

  • Kiryandongo
  • Lamwo
  • Kitgum
  • Agago
  • Omoro
  • Gulu

The organisation should confirm the exact current activity status and preferred administrative names before a district map or coverage count is published.

Planned Geographic Expansion

The profile identifies these as areas for future growth, not confirmed current programme locations:

  • West Nile sub-region
  • Mid-Western Uganda
  • Other vulnerable and underserved regions across Uganda
Who We Work With

Inclusive Community Development

TESR-U's profile identifies a range of beneficiary and stakeholder groups. Specific groups served may vary by programme and location.

Women and Girls

Supporting equitable participation and access to opportunities.

Children and Adolescents

Promoting protection, inclusion and safe community environments.

Youth and Young Entrepreneurs

Encouraging skills development, livelihoods and meaningful participation.

Refugees and Host Communities

Recognising shared priorities, social cohesion and resilience.

Smallholder Farmers

Connecting livelihoods with sustainable agricultural practices.

Persons with Disabilities and Older Persons

Promoting equity, accessibility and social inclusion.

Vulnerable and Marginalised Households

Working towards dignity, participation and stronger community resilience.

Community and Local Institutions

Including community-based organisations and local government institutions.

Evidence and Programme Results

The organisational profile supplied for this website does not include verified beneficiary totals, project counts or measured outcome figures. TESR-U will be able to add programme results, case studies and supporting evidence here when approved information is available.
