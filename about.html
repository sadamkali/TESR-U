Our Background

True Era Sustainable Response Uganda

Established in 2024, TESR-U works with communities to respond to socioeconomic, environmental and humanitarian challenges through local ownership, inclusive participation, innovation and strategic partnerships.

TESR-U is registered with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) as a company limited by guarantee without share capital. Registration number: 80044333317562.

A resilient Uganda where empowered communities lead sustainable development, protect the environment, thrive through meaningful partnerships, and restore hope to the hopeless.

Our Vision

To empower communities across Uganda by promoting sustainable practices, fostering inclusive participation, and building strategic partnerships that drive social, economic, and environmental transformation.

Our Mission

TESR-U's profile lists programme areas in Kiryandongo, Lamwo, Kitgum, Agago, Omoro and Gulu, and describes work with both host and refugee communities. Planned expansion areas are presented separately from current programme areas.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Community-Centered

Communities actively participate in identifying challenges and shaping solutions.

Sustainability

Long-term and environmentally responsible solutions support present and future wellbeing.

Integrity

Transparency, accountability, professionalism and ethical conduct guide our actions.

Collaboration

Strategic partnerships, collective action and shared responsibility help drive change.

Innovation

Creativity, learning and adaptive approaches help address emerging challenges.

Equity and Inclusion

We promote meaningful participation and equitable access for marginalised groups.

Resilience

We strengthen the capacity to withstand, adapt to and recover from social, economic and environmental shocks.

TESR-U
